# Record the console logoff delay warning as an issue and close the investigation

## Why

`plan.md` still had "investigate the console client's logoff behaviour". At a same-machine logoff the console player left a `winsrvext` 100
delay warning, and its log had no `attempt.end reason=shutdown`, so we suspected it had not cleaned up.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| The investigation result is recorded as an issue. Its status is unresolved | [`issue_history/0005-콘솔-로그오프-지연-경고.md`](../../issue_history/0005-콘솔-로그오프-지연-경고.md) |
| The console logoff investigation item is removed and the numbering closed up. The player of the GUI logoff measurement is written as `mere-galaxy` | `plan.md` |

- That log has the process counter output of step 4 of `shutdown()`. The console did clean up. `attempt.end reason=shutdown` was missing because the player was already in the lobby after the host's `CLOSE`
- Which control signal arrived and why `winsrvext` 100 raised the delay warning after 5000 milliseconds are unknown. The repository owner decided not to investigate further. Only the GUI ships
- No code or specification changed

## What happened during the work

At first, the missing `attempt.end reason=shutdown` was combined with the `HandlerRoutine` documentation ("only services receive
`CTRL_LOGOFF_EVENT`") and reported as "the console does not clean up at logoff". On that report the owner chose to write the limit into
`concurrency.md`. While editing, the raw log was opened, the process counter output was found, and the owner was told the earlier report was
wrong. The `concurrency.md` edit in progress was reverted. The owner then chose to record it as an issue and close it.
