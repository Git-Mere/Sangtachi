# Move the demo host to the owner's home Wi-Fi

## Why

On the rehearsal day, Tailscale was blocked on the school wired LAN so other machines could not be handled remotely, and the host dropped when the
owner left with the hotspot. The repository owner changed the demo networks.

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| Host G on the owner's home Wi-Fi, owner A on the mobile hotspot at school, the demo player and the friend on their own home Wi-Fi. This reverses the demo networks of ADR 0034 | [ADR 0038](../../decisions/0038-put-the-demo-host-on-home-wi-fi.md) |
| Wrote that the 3.3 rehearsal used the demo networks of that time | `experiments.md` 3.3 |
| Added the rehearsal of the new demo networks as remaining | `field-test/required-test.md`, `plan.md` |
| G is left at home with nobody there. The Minecraft server is turned off. If the friend takes part, his account goes on the whitelist | `plan.md` |
