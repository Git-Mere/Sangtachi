# The field measurement list `field-test/required-test.md` and the kit tag

## Why

The repository owner decided to do Phase 6 first and the two-home measurements after it. That needed two things: pinning the kit for the
friend to the build at the end of Phase 5, and keeping the measurements people have to do in one place, always up to date, like
`plan.md`. The owner asked for one file `field-test/required-test.md` that keeps being updated and gets reorganized when measurement data
comes back.

## Important decisions

- `plan.md` stays the entry to the remaining work. Measurements are another axis (as roadmap holds implementation) and that table points
  at `required-test.md`. The measurement rows were moved and removed so the same item is not in two files
- The document table in `CLAUDE.md` (`plan.md` holds "waiting work") was not changed. It is a rules file and needs the owner's approval
- What to fix after the results arrive is written down too. The lobby dwell result is pointed at by `concurrency.md` chapter 7 and is
  decided together with the "timing" item that `roadmap.md` does not have
