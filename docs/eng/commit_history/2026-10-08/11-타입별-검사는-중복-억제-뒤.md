# Order of Per-Type Checks in the Receive Pipeline (ADR 0021)

## Why

The wire codec (A) and the acceptance window (B) of Phase 4 wave 1 pointed at the same document gap. Whether the 5.7 `ROSTER` receive
checks and the 5.6 `CLOSE` reason check come before or after the 8.2 epoch checks and 8.3 duplicate suppression was missing. Whether a
packet dropped after passing 8.3 is recorded in the acceptance window was not written as a sentence either. It had to be settled before
the wave 2 receive pipeline. The differences were laid out case by case, and the repository owner chose the recommendation (after 8.3).

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| An order paragraph at the head of section 8. 8.1, 8.2, 8.3, then the per-type checks | `protocol.md` section 8 |
| A packet that passed 8.3 is still recorded in the acceptance window even if dropped later | `protocol.md` 8.3 |
| The 5.6 reason check and the 5.7 receive validation point to the section 8 order | `protocol.md` 5.6, 5.7 |
| `ROSTER` in the valid-packet list of 9.4 and the validation list of 10.4 (a) | `protocol.md` 9.4, 10.4 |
| New ADR | `decisions/0021` |
| Removed two closed rows, and aligned the wave 1 and wave 2 rows of "next" with the current progress (D merges together with D2, one agent for wave 2) | `plan.md` |
