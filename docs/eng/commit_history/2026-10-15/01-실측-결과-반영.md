# Field-test results: the field-test folder and ADR 0024

## Why

On 2026-10-14, `tools/field-test` test 1 (two different homes) and test 2 (same router) were run. Checking the results
raised three things.

- Field data was scattered across `tools/nat-probe/results`, `records`, `tools/nat-lifetime/results`, and the user
  folder. The repository owner asked to keep field measurements in one folder, by date, as reports in one template
- The repository owner decided the two pending decisions in `plan.md` (host NAT warning, and two documented behaviors
  seen in the field)
- With the re-measurement done, one paragraph of `plan.md` was stale

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|------|
| One field folder `field-test/`. Preparation and test procedures (moved from `tools/field-test/README.md`), report template `TEMPLATE.md`, and per date one report per measurement plus a raw-data folder | `field-test/` |
| In `field-test/` only `README.md` and `TEMPLATE.md` are tracked. Reports and raw data stay local (ADR 0001 follow-up action 2) | `.gitignore` |
| Removed the nat-probe-only record template and the empty `records/`. Reports use `field-test/TEMPLATE.md` | `tools/nat-probe/` |
| Field records now live in `field-test/` | `windows-prereq.md`, `stun_client.hpp` comment |
| `ROSTER` receive check 3 becomes "not less than". An equal `generation` is accepted as a resend, and the `roster` line is emitted only for the first or a larger one | `protocol.md` 5.7, `packet.cpp`, `packet.hpp`, `session.cpp` |
| One session's counter table can appear twice and analysis uses the last one | `architecture.md` Chapter 9 |
| No host NAT warning | `roadmap.md` Phase 4 |
| The decisions above and the rejected alternatives | ADR 0024 |
| Phase 3 done, next task is the two remaining parts of test 1 (lobby stay of 3 minutes or more, `a-ports.txt`), lobby-stay wait updated, one finished wait removed | `plan.md` |

Local only: 10 reports and raw data from September to 10-14. The six September nat-probe reports were condensed from
the existing long records into the template by subagents, and the numbers were checked against the JSON.

## Important decisions

- The first proposal was to "silently discard" an equal `generation`, but that conflicts with Chapter 8's "a dedicated
  counter for every discard", so it became "accept it, nothing changes". The decision is ADR 0024
- Field reports are not committed. This follows ADR 0001's policy of not putting the two homes' measurement times and
  records into the public repository
