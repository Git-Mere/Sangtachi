# Split the test run into default, `-Full`, and `-Slow`

## Why

The repository owner said `scripts/test.ps1` took too long. One run took 11 to 12 minutes. First we investigated what each
test judges, whether it overlaps the unit tests, and where its time goes. The owner approved every recommendation of that report.

- 93% of the time was `session-check` (493 s) and `lobby-check` (158 s). Both wait for protocol timers in real time
- No test was found that could be deleted. Many scenes of the two scripts are the only end-to-end place that judges a `roadmap.md` item
- The 665 ctest cases had no tests of removed features and no duplicates either

## What changed

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| The default run is the build, the unit tests, `cli-check`, and `e2e-check`. `-Full` adds the representative cases of the two check scripts. `-Slow` includes `-Full` and runs the remaining cases and the 20-sample RTT baseline. `-Full` and `-Slow` are rejected together with `-Filter` or `-Asan` | `scripts/test.ps1` |
| When to run `-Full` and `-Slow` | `CLAUDE.md`, the `sangtachi-validation` skill, `README.md` |

Of the five definite-error combinations it covers `join_room room_full` and `create_room rate_limited`. Of the eight transient-error combinations it covers `create_room internal x2` and `join_room unavailable x3`. The wait for a second `rate_limited` after `rate_limited` is only in `-Slow` | `scripts/lobby-check.ps1` |

- Product code, product timers, sample counts, and pass conditions did not change
- Waits that check "nothing goes out" (`host-report-stop` 7 s, no retry 2.5 s, no fourth request 2 s) were not shortened. They cannot become condition waits
- The periodic `host_report` wait of `departed-now` stays. It is the check that separates the immediate report from the periodic one
- `loop-alive` stays in the default run. It is the only end-to-end place for the `[loop]` stall check
- The build folder file `C:\me\build\test\Debug\.ninja_deps` was corrupt, so 33 steps were rebuilt even with no change. It was deleted with the owner's approval. It is outside the repository. After that the second build takes 2.0 s and is `ninja: no work to do`

## What happened during the work

Left in, that scene would always fail. The verification above was rerun from the start after the removal
